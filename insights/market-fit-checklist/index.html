Five questions to answer before shipping to the GCCSample

20 October 2026

This is a sample post to show the layout. Replace it with a real KNS Uncles article before publishing.

Before a first meeting with a Gulf distributor, a Korean brand should be able to answer these questions.

1. Is the product allowed as it is?

Check ingredients, claims and labelling against the target country’s rules before anything else.

2. Who registers the product?

Decide whether the brand, the distributor or a third party holds the registration.

3. Which channel fits?

Pharmacy, beauty retail and online each need a different price structure.

4. What does the distributor earn?

A deal that leaves no margin for the partner will not last past the first order.

5. Who handles shipping and payment terms?

Agree Incoterms and payment terms early so the first order is not delayed.
