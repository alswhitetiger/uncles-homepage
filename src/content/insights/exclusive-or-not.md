Exclusive rights are often the first thing a distributor asks for. Whether to grant them depends on what the partner commits to in return.

## When exclusivity helps

When the distributor commits to registration, marketing spend and minimum orders, exclusivity gives them a reason to invest.

## When it hurts

Without performance terms, exclusivity can lock a brand out of a market for years.

## A middle path

Time-limited exclusivity tied to minimum volumes keeps both sides committed.
